DAMAGE CALCULATION FORMULA:

Damage = [(Attack * Bonus Multiplier) / Defense] * 25 + Flat Bonus

Bonus Multiplier: terrain and type advantage bonuses
Flat Bonus: Heroes

VILLAGER: Untrained civilian unit. Equipped with farming tools and no armor.
    COST: 50 food
    ATTACK: 50
    DEFENSE: 0
    AGE: 1

SPEARMEN: Basic military unit; villagers called upon to serve in the army. Advantageous against
light cavalry. Weak against ranged units and heavy infantry. Equipped with spears, and leather 
armor (light armor). LIGHT INFANTRY
    COST: 50 food, 50 wood
    ATTACK: 100
    DEFENSE: 100
    AGE: 1

SWORDSMEN: Trained military unit, soldiers who have spent some time in battle. Advantageous against
ranged units and light infantry. Weak against cavalry. Equipped with swords, sheilds, and chainmail
armor (medium armor). HEAVY INFANTRY
    COST: 75 food, 75 gold
    ATTACK: 150
    DEFENSE: 150
    AGE: 2

TEMPLAR KNIGHT: Elite infantry unit, veterens with elite skill on the battle field. Advantageous
against ranged units and light infantry. Weak against cavalry. Equipped with swords, shields and
plated armor (heavy). ELITE INFANTRY
    COST: 130 food, 150 gold
    ATTACK: 300
    DEFENSE: 300
    AGE: 4

ARCHERS: Trained ranged unit. Advantageous against light infantry. Weak against cavalry and heavy 
infantry. Equipped with bows, and leather armor (light). RANGED

    DEFENSE: 100
    AGE: 2
